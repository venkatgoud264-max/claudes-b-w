Our Philosophy

Mastery in
monochrome

We don't chase trends. We've spent years perfecting a single, radical idea: that a t-shirt — worn closest to the skin, felt most intimately — should be the finest garment you own.

Our fabric is sourced from single-origin Supima cotton grown in the Konkan coast, Maharashtra. Each tee is cut to sub-millimetre tolerances by master craftspeople in our Mumbai atelier.

Single-Origin
Supima Cotton

Grown on a single farm along Maharashtra's Konkan coast. Traceable from seed to stitch, with a fibre 45% softer than standard cotton.

02

Engineered
Drape

200g/m² fabric with a structured yet fluid hand-feel. The weight creates a silhouette that holds its form across decades of wear.

03

Mumbai
Atelier

Cut and sewn by master craftspeople in our atelier in Dharavi, Mumbai — established 1981. Eight artisans. Every piece hand-numbered.

04

Permanent
Colour

Reactive-dyed in small batches using low-impact inks. The black won't grey. The white won't yellow. We guarantee it for 5 years.
